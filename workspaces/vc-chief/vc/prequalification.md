# Pre-Qualification

> [MUST_CUSTOMIZE] Set stage/thesis-specific minimum evidence and research
> promotion thresholds from review capacity and labeled cases. Do not convert
> missing early-stage metrics into negative evidence.

Policy version: `3.0`

Prequalification controls research cost; it is not a final investment decision.

## Minimum

Require reliable company identity, source/origin record, one thesis-relevant evidence item, memory lookup, trust decision, and no hard exclusion. Submitted documents can establish a claim but not verified quality.

## Outcomes

- `pass`: hard exclusion or clearly outside thesis;
- `watch`: identifiable and plausible but below deeper-research evidence;
- `research_deeper`: minimum is met and a bounded specialist question remains;
- `high_priority`: only after the customized Version 3 rubric reaches its
  configured threshold with no override and all higher-evidence prerequisites;
- `needs_human_review`: approval, confidentiality, legal/identity, or blocking contradiction;
- `insufficient_evidence`: identity or minimum coverage absent.

Origin does not add score. Sparse outbound data is not a penalty beyond missing evidence, and rich inbound claims are not a bonus. Return evidence IDs, missing states, hard-exclusion result, proposed profile/agents, approval need, and rationale.
