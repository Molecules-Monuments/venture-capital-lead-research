## Purpose

This is the fixed-source watchlist for passive and recurring outbound discovery.

## Source classes

| Class | Examples | Cadence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company blogs | Technical/product blogs | weekly | Watch launch and technical depth. |
| VC portfolios | Competitor VC, angels, upstream funds | weekly | Use only public portfolio pages. |
| News/RSS | TechCrunch, Sifted, Hacker News, relevant newsletters | daily/weekly | Preserve source URL and date. |
| Press releases | Company and wire releases | weekly | Treat as company claims unless independently verified. |
| Research reports | Public PDFs from firms/universities | monthly | Route PDFs through document intake. |
| Open source | GitHub orgs, package registries | weekly | Use public metrics only. |
| Job boards | Company career pages | weekly | Hiring is a signal, not proof of revenue. |
