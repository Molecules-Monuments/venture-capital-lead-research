# Inbound Sources

> [MUST_CUSTOMIZE] Define authenticated source identities, trust assumptions,
> consent/lawful purpose, allowed fields, retention, and downstream consumers.

Policy version: `3.0`; source taxonomy retained from the 2026-06-10 ground source and subordinate to Version 3 trust/document/approval controls.

## Inbound channels

- Founder email.
- Website form.
- Partner or VC referral.
- Angel or operator referral.
- Warm intro.
- Event follow-up.
- Uploaded deck or spreadsheet.

## Required inbound triage

For every inbound lead, capture:

- Submitter or referrer.
- Relationship to the company.
- Date received.
- Documents received.
- Claims made.
- Missing fields.
- Consent or confidentiality notes, if provided.

## Rule

Inbound does not mean high quality. Treat inbound material as claims until verified.
