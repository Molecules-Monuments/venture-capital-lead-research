# Version 3.0 research index

All research was completed and saved before the Version 3.0 runtime was
materialized or amended. The order was deliberately specialist-first so the
orchestrator was assessed against actual worker boundaries rather than an
imagined organization chart.

## Method and broad practitioner roster

- `00_SOURCE_METHOD_AND_ROSTER.md` — evidence grades, marketing/outcome
  cautions, top-tier and contrast fund list, investors, founders/operators,
  academic/standards spine, and the operational translation rule.

The roster is not a league table. Fund return, portfolio outcome, and practice
exemplar are different labels. A public success story never supplies a local
score weight.

skills, compares multiple human/practitioner approaches, includes independent
or academic counterevidence and transfer limits, rejects unsafe/non-causal
imports, and maps proposed changes to an evaluation frozen before edits.

## Orchestration and memory

- `agents/12-vc-chief-orchestrator.md` — written after all eleven worker

  evidence integration, dissent, budgets, and stop rules.
- `13-memory-retrieval.md` — end-to-end audit of Postgres lookup and OpenClaw

## Decision files outside research

- `../00_RESEARCH_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` — research and work plan plus
  adversarial review of the method.
- `../01_PRECOMMITTED_EVALS.md` — immutable pre-change quality gates.
- `../02_BASELINE_ASSESSMENT_AND_CHANGE_GATE.md` — document/system assessment,
  causal change register, rejected changes, and rollback triggers.

Runtime policy must not cite “the research says” as authority. A dossier change
is adopted only when it affects a bounded contract/mechanism, has a local eval,
and preserves the stated security invariants.
